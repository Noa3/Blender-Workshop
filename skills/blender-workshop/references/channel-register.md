Across the earlier twenty channels, 5,342 Videos entries and 2,760 Shorts entries were listed. The public tabs were followed to their end; @BlenderTutorialBT had no Shorts tab. The repeated @BlendTweaks link was counted once. This is a snapshot of the public Videos and Shorts tabs, not a claim about private videos or livestreams.

Individual notes cover 97 Videos entries and 38 Shorts. A note may rest on narration, selected player stills or creator text: it does not mean full playback. The other 7,967 entries have no individual workshop note. This edition includes the written creator notes, not the historical channel catalog or any copied videos.
